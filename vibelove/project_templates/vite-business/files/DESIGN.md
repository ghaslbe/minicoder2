# Design

- Compact business application, not a landing page. Use the existing layout.
- White surfaces, graphite text, teal primary actions, amber pending states.
- Reuse PageHeader, DataTable, EntitySheet and ConfirmDelete.
- Lists belong in tables. Create/edit belongs in a sheet. Confirm deletion.
- Keep visible labels, keyboard focus, empty/error/loading states and mobile layouts.
- Use lucide-react icons with accessible names and title tooltips for icon buttons.
- No decorative gradients, hero sections or oversized headings.
- Adapt the customer example to the requested domain; do not regenerate the scaffold.
